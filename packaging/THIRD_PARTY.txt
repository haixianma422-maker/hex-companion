The application folder includes Python, Qt 6 with PySide6, Pillow, RapidOCR,
ONNX Runtime, OpenCV, NumPy, pystray and their runtime dependencies.

Upstream source:
Python: https://www.python.org/downloads/source/
Qt: https://download.qt.io/official_releases/qt/
PySide6 / Qt for Python: https://download.qt.io/official_releases/QtForPython/
Pillow: https://github.com/python-pillow/Pillow
RapidOCR: https://github.com/RapidAI/RapidOCR
ONNX Runtime: https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime
OpenCV Python: https://github.com/opencv/opencv-python
NumPy: https://github.com/numpy/numpy
pystray: https://github.com/moses-palmer/pystray

Qt 6.12 and PySide6 6.12 are used under LGPL-3.0. They are shipped unmodified
as separate shared libraries in _internal/PySide6 and _internal/shiboken6, and
can be replaced with compatible builds. The LGPL-3.0 and GPL-3.0 texts are in
licenses/LGPL-3.0.txt and licenses/GPL-3.0.txt.

pystray 0.19.5 is distributed under LGPL-3.0.
Its unmodified installed Python source is also included in licenses/pystray-source/.
The companion source package includes its PyInstaller spec and build script,
so developers can rebuild with a replacement dependency.

OCR model origin/configuration is recorded in RapidOCR's default_models.yaml
inside the executable. The application uses the bundled PP-OCRv6 detection
and recognition models; model inference does not require a network download.

OP.GG snapshot files record their source URLs and retrieval timestamps.
Equipment icons are Riot Games Data Dragon assets. Their version and source
are recorded in data/item-icons/manifest.json in the source repository.
Game text metadata is sourced from CommunityDragon.
Base champion art, portraits, ability and summoner icons use Riot Games Data
Dragon assets. Tencent provides champion art fallbacks and augment icons.
Two augment icons use CommunityDragon's extracted Riot Games assets.
Their URLs are recorded in data/visual_catalog.json; the cached resource
inventory and SHA-256 hashes are in visual-assets/resource-cache-manifest.json.
OP.GG data and Riot Games assets remain subject to their owners' terms;
the project's MIT license does not grant rights to those third-party resources.
